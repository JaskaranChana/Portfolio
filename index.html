About

01

I build Android applications that people trust with their money. Over the last five years I've shipped production mobile software at Comviva and Rupeek — fintech products serving millions of users across Africa, Asia, and India — and contributed to the release engineering, performance and security practices those products depend on.

My day-to-day work lives in Kotlin, Jetpack Compose and Flutter, with deep exposure to MVVM, dependency injection, coroutines, and the CI/CD surface around them — Jenkins, Firebase distribution, SonarQube, Checkmarx. I care about codebases that stay readable under pressure and teams that ship without breaking trust.

Since 2024 I've also been working as an AI Training Specialist at Outlier AI, fine-tuning large language models for code generation and reasoning. The work involves designing evaluation suites, surfacing failure modes in production model outputs, and collaborating with research teams on retraining cycles.

I hold an M.Eng in Electrical & Computer Engineering from the University of Windsor and a B.Tech from NIT Jalandhar. I'm based in Toronto and open to senior engineering roles — on-site, hybrid, or remote.
